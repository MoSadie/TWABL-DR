## Upcoming Portal Updates

### Previously Announced Reward Buffs - Coming Sept 9.

- Several Fireteam Ops activities will have an increase in the number of drops
- Activity timers have been rebalanced across the board
- Players will have a chance for a Prime Engram drop per Portal Activity completion reward
- The Portal Timer bonus earned will grant 5% minimum score

## Trials Rewards

The weekly featured weapon rotation returns! The Trials weekly featured weapon can be acquired with each visit to the Lighthouse and will have a chance to drop in addition to all pre-existing rewards when completing a match, win or lose. Drop chance varies based on Grade, with C+ scaling to 20% and A+ scaling to 60%

More details in next week's TWID.

## 9.1.0.1 Portal Buffs

These changes are coming the next (normal) update AFTER Ash & Iron. This info is still WIP.

- Removing Grappler, Hypercritical, Oscillation, and Match Game from matchmade Portal experiences.
- From 500-550 Power, earning an A or A+ will give you a +1 Power on your activity completion rewards.
    - This change will also be in effect for A grades in the 400-450 Power range when Renegades launches.
- Improving Fireteam Ops rewards. Plans are being finalized, more details soon.
- **NEW**: GM Difficulty Portal activities will no longer zero out revives when you hit the time limit. (Unchanged for GM Conquests)
- **NEW**: Make Tier 5 rewards easier to earn in the Portal:
    - From 450 Power onwards (Platinum or Diamond reward packages) earn at least 1,250,000 points to guarantee Tier 5 drops. (Use the score/reward prediction to verify.)
    - As you go up in Power, you can earn Tier 5 rewards with lower grades. (Ex 500 Power, only needs a B+)

More is planned for the future, the work isn't done.

### New Pinnacle Ops: Dungeon Lairs

These are dungeon encounters but with Portal modifiers, scoring, and loot. Will be available in the Pinnacle Ops section.

The three Dungeon Lairs being added are:
- Pit Of Heresy: Beneath the Necropolis (First Encounter)
- Duality: Nightmare of Gahlran (First Boss Encounter)
- Warlord's Ruin: Hefnd's Vengeance (Final Boss Encounter)

In addition to the normal Portal rewards, owners of the corresponding Dungeon Key can enable the Classic Collector modifier to earn legacy dungeon rewards as well. (Please note, they will not have Seasonal Power or Gear Tiers)

#### Free Dungeon Access Event

For the first two weeks after Ash & Iron launches (9/9 - 9/23), all players will have free access to the both the *original* dungeons and the Classic Collector modifier. All players can always access the new Dungeon Lairs in Pinnacle Ops, but the original dungeons will require owning the corresponding Dungeon Key after the event ends.

### A Few More Things

#### 9.1.0 - Ash & Iron Launch

- Fireteam Power will no longer adjust your score when below 300 Power. This means playing with friends are at lower Power than you will improve their rewards, allowing quicker access to Tier 2 gear
- A "Discard" action as been added to Portal engrams when in the Postmaster, allowing quicker dismantles

#### 9.5.0 - Renegades Launch

- When Renegades launches, all Legendary gear from Season: Reclamation (The Edge of Fate and Ash & Iron) will be included as part of Season: Lawless Featured Gear.
- All new Exotics from Season: Reclamation will also be included as part of Season: Lawless Featured Gear.
- The New Gear Bonus will be changing for Season: Lawless Artifact:
    - Starting in Season: Lawless, the New Gear Bonus will be a flat bonus for all Featured Gear. It will no longer be tiered as it is currently in Season: Reclamation and will be a max 5% damage for weapons and a max 10% damage resistance for armor.

## Player Support Report

### Claim Your Seasonal Challenges Before Tuesday!

When Ash & Iron launches next week, Seasonal Challenges will be refreshed. Players should claim any complete challenges they have before the game goes down for maintenance on Tuesday.

### Upcoming Issue With Displayed Gear Power

Due to an issue, Update 9.1.0 will include an issue where some gear may display a different number instead of their Power. When this happens, the item’s displayed Power will match a stat on the item, and it can’t be updated with infusion. This is purely a visual issue and the item’s actual Power will not be affected. Your access to content, difficulty when facing combatants, and rewards will also not be impacted. The cause of this issue has been identified, and a fix is planned for an upcoming update.

### Known Issues List

- Dying against the Exile Ranniks boss on Kepler’s Mythic difficulty when trying to complete the steps for Graviton Spike's Transcendent Zen Catalyst causes the boss to despawn.
- Some players are unable to interact with a briefcase during the Declassification side quest.
- Some platform achievements currently can’t be completed.
- The countdown timer for Guardian Ranks is using the Rewards Pass timer.
    - Note: Guardian Ranks are *not* being reset when Ash & Iron launches.
